This page is for people who joined someone else's workspace through a link, as a guest. If you're a member
with your own account there, you can erase what you wrote yourself, straight away.

## Ask

Open the conversation you were invited to, and choose **Ask \{workspace\} to erase what you wrote here**. You can
add a note for the owner. Then send it.

You'll see:

> Your request is with \{workspace\}. They confirm it before anything is erased. Keep this page's link to see what happens.

**Keep that page's link.** It's the only way to see the outcome, and it keeps working after your access to the
workspace ends. Nobody else is given it, and the workspace keeps only a scrambled form it can't read back, so nobody can
give you a new one. Keep it somewhere safe.

If you asked already, asking again finds your first request. If the link you're using can't make a request,
you'll see:

> We couldn't take this request from this link. If you asked already, it's with \{workspace\}.

## What the owner does

Nothing is erased until the workspace's owner answers. They see your request, with your note, and choose
**Erase** or **Don't erase**. If they choose not to, they can say why.

## See what happened

Open the link you kept. It shows one of these:

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| "Waiting for \{workspace\} to confirm. Asked \{date\}." | The owner hasn't answered. |
| "Erased. Here's what went, what was kept, and why." | It's done. The page lists what was erased and anything that was kept, with the reason for each. |
| "Erasing was interrupted. It finishes by itself, and this page shows what's left." | The erase started and hasn't finished. Come back to the same link. |
| "\{workspace\} didn't erase this. Their reason: “\{reason\}”" | The owner chose not to, and said why. |
| "\{workspace\} didn't erase this, and didn't give a reason." | The owner chose not to. |

## What can be kept

An erase can't always reach everything, and the page says so instead of leaving it out. Each line says what
was kept and why:

| Line | Why |
|---|---|
| "Kept: \{n\} records written before this workspace could erase them. …" | They were written before the workspace sealed what people write. Your words in them are hidden wherever they're shown, and the line says which backups still hold them. |
| "Kept: \{n\} things you wrote here that aren't messages, such as notes or table rows. …" | Whether these are erased on request hasn't been decided, so they stay. |
| "Kept as markers, with your words removed: \{n\} records other people rely on." | Other people's records point to them, so a marker stays with your words gone. |
| "Kept unchanged, including what you wrote: \{n\} records other people rely on." | Records others rely on that couldn't be changed without breaking them. |
| "Kept while a hold is in place. The reason given: “\{reason\}”" | The owner is keeping them for a reason they state, such as a legal obligation. |
| "Kept: \{n\} records of a kind this workspace hasn't said how to erase. The owner has been told." | The workspace doesn't know how to erase this kind of record, so it's kept and the owner is told. |

Copies made before your request, such as an export someone saved, aren't reached by an erase.

## If you lost the link

If you haven't asked yet, the workspace's owner can file the request for you and send you a link to follow it.
If you already asked, your request stands and the owner still answers it, but the link can't be replaced.
