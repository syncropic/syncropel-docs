---
title: Set up your organization
description: Create a workspace for your organization, pay for Pro, and claim your organization's name with your domain. About fifteen minutes, most of it waiting for DNS.
---

This page takes you from nothing to an organization workspace with its own name, such as `acme`, that other workspaces can find. If you're setting up a workspace just for yourself, you want [Create your workspace](/docs/get-started/hosted) instead.

## What you'll need

- **An email address for the organization**, not a person's own. The workspace belongs to whoever signs up, so an address the organization controls (such as `accounts@acme.com`) means the workspace doesn't depend on one person's account. The people who work in it join as members.
- **A card for Pro.** An organization workspace is on Pro, paid as you create it. See [Plans and billing](/docs/get-started/pricing).
- **Your organization's domain**, such as `acme.com`, and access to its DNS settings. The name you claim comes from the domain: `acme.com` gives you `acme`.

## The steps

```
 syncropel.com      Who is it for?       pay for Pro      setting up       account.syncropel.com
┌──────────┐      ┌──────────────┐     ┌──────────┐     ┌──────────┐     ┌──────────────────────┐
│ Sign up  │ ───▶ │An organization│ ──▶ │ Start Pro│ ──▶ │name, ✓ ✓ │ ──▶ │ Names: claim `acme`  │
└──────────┘      └──────────────┘     └──────────┘     └──────────┘     │ add the DNS record   │
                                                                         │ Check and claim      │
                                                                         └──────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Sign up, and say it's for an organization

1. Go to [syncropel.com](https://syncropel.com) and sign up with the organization's email address.
2. At **Who is it for?**, choose **An organization**. Do it here: the pages after this don't ask again.

### 2. Pay for Pro

Press **Start Pro**. You go to checkout, and come back to set up the workspace when the payment is done.

### 3. Name the workspace, and wait for it

Back from checkout, type your workspace's address, such as `acme.syncropel.app`, and press **create**. Watch the setup steps tick off. It usually takes a minute or two. You land in your workspace, signed in as its owner.

### 4. Claim your organization's name

1. Go to [account.syncropel.com](https://account.syncropel.com), open **Names**, and press **Claim a name**. Give the name and the domain that proves it.
2. You're shown a DNS record to add at your DNS provider:

   | Field | Value |
   |---|---|
   | Type | `TXT` |
   | Name | `_syncropel` (some providers want the full `_syncropel.acme.com`) |
   | Value | `syncropel-verify=…`, copied exactly as shown |
   | TTL | your provider's default, such as **Auto**, is fine |

   Add it on that name itself, not through a CNAME. In Cloudflare, either form of the name works.
3. Press **Check and claim**. The record usually shows up within a few minutes; some providers take longer. If the check doesn't pass yet, your claim waits under **Waiting for your DNS record**. Press **Continue** to try again later.
4. When the check passes, the name is yours.

### 5. Bring people in

- **Invite people by link** from your workspace's Members. They join as members of the organization's workspace.
- **Add offices and agents under your name**, such as `acme/support`, with **Add a name** on the Names page. Each one points at someone in the workspace, so add them once the person they'll point at has joined.

## Keep the DNS record

Your domain is checked again every day. Leave the record in place. If the check starts failing, you can't add anything new under the name until it passes again; nothing you already have is taken away. See [Organization names](/docs/guides/organization-names) for the rules: what a name can look like, protected names, and moving or retiring offices.
