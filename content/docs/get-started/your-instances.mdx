title: Your workspaces
description: You can own several workspaces, each with its own permanent address. This page explains how that works and how to reach each one.
---

A Syncropel **workspace** is a complete place of its own, with its own records, threads, files and API. You are not limited to one. You can own **several workspaces**. Each has a permanent address, and you stay the owner of every one.

## One identity, several workspaces

When you sign in, the workspaces you own belong to you, not to a device or a browser. Sign in anywhere and the same workspaces are there. This is the same identity described in [Your identity & recovery](/docs/get-started/identity).

Why own more than one? Common reasons:

- **Separate contexts**: a personal workspace and a work workspace, kept fully isolated.
- **Separate projects**: one workspace per initiative, each with its own content and history.
- **A throwaway**: a scratch workspace to try something without touching your main one.

## Each workspace has a stable URL

Every hosted workspace has its own permanent address:

```
https://<label>.syncropel.app
```

The `<label>` is chosen when the workspace is created and **never changes**: you can bookmark it, share it, or point a client at it, and it will keep working. (Syncropel's own services live under `syncropel.com`; your workspaces live under `syncropel.app`.)

A workspace's address and its owner stay the same for as long as the workspace exists.

## How many

Your first hosted workspace is free, with no card: it includes 1 GB of storage, and a one-time allowance for the assistant. Each workspace after that is $25 per month, with a 30-day free trial (see [Pricing](/docs/get-started/pricing)). Each one is the full product, not a reduced version.

## Reaching and connecting to your workspaces

- **On the web**, sign in and open a workspace to work in it directly.
- **From the `spl` command line**, point it at a workspace's URL to read and add content, or run commands against it: see [Connect the command line](/docs/get-started/connect-the-command-line).
- **Across devices**, the same workspaces are there wherever you sign in.

## Isolation: your workspaces are yours

Your workspaces are separate from each other and from everyone else's. Another person signing in sees **none** of your workspaces or their content, and a key for one workspace never opens another.

## In short

- One identity can own **many** workspaces.
- Each has a **permanent URL** (`<label>.syncropel.app`) you can bookmark and share.
- Each is the **full product**. One is free; each one after that is billed on its own.
- They're **separate**: no one else can reach yours.
