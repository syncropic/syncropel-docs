A Syncropel **workspace** is a complete runtime — its own records, threads, files, and API. You are not limited to one. A single identity can own **several workspaces**, and they're all yours: each has a stable address, and your identity is the permanent owner of every one.

## One identity, several workspaces

When you sign in, the workspaces you own are tied to your identity — not to a device or a browser. Sign in anywhere and the same set of workspaces is yours. This is the same portable identity described in [Your identity & recovery](/docs/get-started/identity): it follows you, and so does everything it owns.

Why own more than one? Common reasons:

- **Separate contexts** — a personal workspace and a work workspace, kept fully isolated.
- **Separate projects** — one workspace per initiative, each with its own content and history.
- **A throwaway** — a scratch workspace to try something without touching your main one.

## Each workspace has a stable URL

Every hosted workspace is reachable at its own permanent address:

```
https://<label>.syncropel.app
```

The `<label>` is chosen when the workspace is created and **never changes** — you can bookmark it, share it, or point a client at it, and it resolves the same way today and a year from now. (Foundational Syncropel services live under `syncropel.com`; your workspaces live under `syncropel.app`.)

That stability is a guarantee, not a convenience: a workspace's address and its ownership are fixed for the life of the workspace. Nothing you build on it ever needs to move.

## How many

A signed-in identity can create as many workspaces as it needs; each is billed on its own (see [Pricing](/docs/get-started/pricing)). Each one is a real, full runtime, not a reduced version of the product.

## Reaching and connecting to your workspaces

- **On the web**, sign in and open a workspace to work in it directly.
- **From the `spl` command line**, point it at a workspace's URL to query, file content, or run commands against it — see [Connecting the CLI](/docs/get-started/pairing).
- **Across devices**, because your identity is portable, the same workspaces are available wherever you sign in.

<Callout>
Switching fluidly between your workspaces inside the web app — a single picker that flips the whole workspace from one to the next — is being rolled out. The ownership model, the URLs, and reaching each workspace directly all work today; the in-app switcher is the convenience layer on top.
</Callout>

## Isolation — your workspaces are yours

Instances are isolated from each other and from everyone else's. Another person signing in sees **none** of your workspaces or their content, and one workspace's credential never grants access to another. Ownership is the boundary, and it's enforced, not assumed.

## In short

- One portable identity owns **many** workspaces.
- Each has a **permanent URL** (`<label>.syncropel.app`) you can bookmark and share.
- Each is a **full runtime**, billed on its own.
- They're **isolated** — yours are yours, and no one else can reach them.
