## The two plans

|  | **Free** | **Pro** |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $0, no card | $25 a month, per workspace |
| How many | One free workspace per person | No set limit |
| Storage | 1 GB | 20 GB |
| The assistant | A starting allowance, once (it doesn't renew) | Use included every month. Past what's included, the assistant pauses until more is available, for example when next month's share begins. |
| A name for your organization | No | Yes, checked against your domain ([Organization names](/docs/guides/organization-names)) |
| Cancelling | Nothing to cancel | Cancel any time; it runs to the end of the period you've paid for |

Both plans give you a workspace at your own address, `<your-name>.syncropel.app`, kept up to date for you, that you open from any browser.

## Where to manage it

Everything about plans and billing is on **[account.syncropel.com](https://account.syncropel.com)**, signed in with the same account you use for your workspace.

```
account.syncropel.com
├── Workspaces      each workspace, its plan, and Move to Pro
├── Names           your organization's name
└── Account
    ├── Billing     your card, invoices and subscription
    ├── Export      a copy of what's yours
    └── Danger      deleting your account
```

## Move a workspace from Free to Pro

1. On [account.syncropel.com](https://account.syncropel.com), open **Workspaces** and choose the workspace.
2. Press **Move to Pro**.
3. Check the price and pay. You come back to the workspace page when the payment is done.

Your workspace stays exactly as it is: the same address, the same people and everything in it. It restarts once while it moves, which takes a moment.

## Your bill

- Pro is charged monthly, per workspace.
- Your card, invoices and subscription are under **Account**, then **Billing**.
- Assistant use on Pro is included. When the month's share is used, the assistant pauses until more is available, for example when next month's share begins; nothing is charged on top.

## Running Syncropel on your own computer

You can also run a workspace on your own computer or server for free. That's a technical setup, covered in the developer docs: [Install spl](/docs/get-started/install) and [Moving between your computer and hosted](/docs/guides/portability).
