Your workspace is your own place on Syncropel: your conversations, tasks, files, decisions and assistant, at an address that's yours. We run it for you, so there's nothing to install or keep running.

Your first workspace is **free**, with no card. See [Plans and billing](/docs/get-started/pricing).

## What you'll need

- An email address, or a Google or other social sign-in.
- A name for your workspace's address. If you pick `alice`, your workspace lives at `alice.syncropel.app`. Use 4 to 32 lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, starting with a letter and not ending with a hyphen.

That's all.

## Sign up

```
  syncropel.com          pick your address          a minute or two          your workspace
 ┌─────────────┐        ┌──────────────────┐        ┌───────────────┐        ┌──────────────┐
 │   Sign up   │ ─────▶ │ alice            │ ─────▶ │ setting up... │ ─────▶ │  the Studio  │
 │             │        │ .syncropel.app   │        │ ✓ ✓ ✓ ✓       │        │  (you're in) │
 └─────────────┘        └──────────────────┘        └───────────────┘        └──────────────┘
```

1. Go to [syncropel.com](https://syncropel.com) and sign up. Confirm your email if asked.
2. Type the name you want for your address. If it's taken or kept back for Syncropel's own use, you'll see that straight away; pick another.
3. Watch the setup steps tick off. It usually takes a minute or two.
4. You land in your workspace, in the **Studio**, signed in as its owner. There are no keys to copy.

## Your workspaces, from any device

Sign in at [account.syncropel.com](https://account.syncropel.com) and open **Workspaces** to see every workspace you own, its plan and its status, and to open any of them in the Studio. Sign in on a new device and the same list is there.

Want another workspace for a separate project? Create another. Your first is free; each one after that is on [Pro](/docs/get-started/pricing).

## Your address

`<your-name>.syncropel.app` is your workspace's front door. Opened on its own, it shows a public page with your workspace's name; you work in the Studio. The address stays the same for as long as you keep the workspace, and it can't be renamed yet.

## If setup doesn't finish

- **The name is taken.** Someone chose it first. Pick another.
- **It's taking longer than usual.** Wait half a minute and try again from the same page; your workspace may already be running.

If it still doesn't work, the page shows details you can send to support.
