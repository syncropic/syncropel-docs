A hosted workspace is one you created at [syncropel.com](https://syncropel.com). It runs for you
in the cloud, so there is nothing to install and nothing to keep running. This page explains what
happens to your work while you use it, while you are away, and if you ever delete it.

If you have not created one yet, start with [Create your workspace](/docs/get-started/hosted).

## A saved copy of your workspace

Every hosted workspace, on Free and on Pro, keeps a **saved copy** of itself away from the machine it runs on. You don't have to do anything: there's no key to look after and no button to press. The workspace saves new work to its copy on its own, for as long as it exists.

```
   your workspace ──── new work, within seconds ────▶  saved copy
   (where you work)                                    (kept away from it)
```

## How often your work is saved

| Plan | New work is saved off the machine within about |
|---|---|
| Free | 30 seconds |
| Pro | 10 seconds |

If saving is ever paused, your workspace tells you.

See [Plans and billing](/docs/get-started/pricing) for what each plan includes.

## When your workspace sleeps

A hosted workspace goes to sleep when nobody is using it, and wakes up when you open it again.
Waking usually takes a couple of seconds.

Nothing is lost while it sleeps. When it wakes, it picks up saving where it left off.

If you use the command line and see a connection error right after a quiet period, wait a moment
and try again: the workspace is probably waking up.

## Scheduled work

Scheduled work (a daily summary, a weekly check) runs while your workspace is awake. See [Scheduled triggers](/docs/guides/scheduled-triggers) for how schedules are set up.

## What the saved copy holds

- your conversations, messages and decisions
- your files, up to your plan's file allowance
- your assistants, their settings and their schedules
- your workspace's settings

## Pausing or deleting a workspace

You'll find both on [account.syncropel.com](https://account.syncropel.com): open **Workspaces**, choose the workspace, and look under **Settings**.

### Suspend: a pause with a way back

**Suspend** stops the workspace. You have **7 days** to change your mind and press **Resume**. We email you a day before the 7 days end. If you don't resume it, the workspace is deleted when the 7 days are up, exactly as below.

### Delete

**Delete** removes the workspace straight away, and its address becomes free for someone else to take.

If the workspace kept a saved copy, that copy stays for **30 days** so you can still start a new workspace from it. You'll see it under **Repositories** on account.syncropel.com, with the date it was saved and its size. After 30 days it's deleted.

When you delete, you can tick **Also delete its kept records now** to have the copy deleted at once instead. Either way, a snapshot of the workspace's disk is kept for up to 7 days, then deleted.

```
 Suspend ─────── 7 days (email 1 day before the end) ───────┐
    │                                                       ▼
    └── Resume: back as it was                           Deleted
                                                            │
 Delete ────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
                                                            ▼
                          saved copy kept 30 days, then gone
                          (or gone at once if you ticked
                           "Also delete its kept records now")
```

Delete each workspace before deleting your account. **Export** under **Account** gives you a copy of what's yours first.

## Taking your work with you

Your workspace is never locked in. You can export everything at any time and run it on your own
machine, or move work from your own machine into a hosted workspace. See
[Portability](/docs/guides/portability) (a guide for developers).
