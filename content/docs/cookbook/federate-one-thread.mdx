---
title: Federate one thread
description: Share one task thread with a colleague who runs their own workspace, and nothing else.
---

## Problem

A teammate runs their own workspace. You're working on a task (say `TASK-0042`), and you want them to see its records as they arrive. Their workspace should fetch the new records for that thread and check their signatures. You do *not* want to share everything, just this one thread. They should not see your trust data, your other tasks, your settings, or any other thread.

## Recipe

You have both already run `spl init` and have an identity (a DID). Exchange identities and addresses, then set up one pair for the thread ID.

On **your** side, you share the thread and they follow it:

```bash
# 1. Get the thread ID for the task.
TASK_THREAD=$(spl task show TASK-0042 -o json | jq -r '.thread')
echo "$TASK_THREAD"
# th_a1b2c3d4…

# 2. Tell them your identity and address. They need both.
spl identity show -o json | jq '{did, endpoint_url}'
# { "did": "did:sync:user:alice", "endpoint_url": "https://alice.example.com:9100" }
```

On **their** side, they add a pair that fetches from you:

```bash
# They need your identity, your address, and the thread ID.
spl fleet sync add "did:sync:user:alice" "$TASK_THREAD" \
  --peer-url "https://alice.example.com:9100"

# Check the pair was added and is fetching.
spl fleet sync list

# Fetch right now (instead of waiting for the next automatic fetch).
spl fleet sync kick <pair-id>
```

Within a few seconds of every record you add to `TASK-0042`, their workspace receives it. Signatures are checked against your identity, and consent is checked too: if you and your teammate are in different namespaces, nothing is shared unless a consent grant exists.

## The trade-off

**Each pair covers one thread.** If you start another thread (for example, the thread where an agent works on `TASK-0042`), your teammate won't see it, because their pair follows only the first. To share more threads, they add another pair for each one.

**Sharing across namespaces needs a consent grant.** If your task is in `default` and theirs is in `their-namespace`, records don't cross without a grant on `th_consent` naming the source, the target and what may be shared. See [Consent management](/docs/guides/consent) for the grant flow.

**Your side doesn't send anything.** Your workspace doesn't know their pair exists. They fetch on their own schedule (every few seconds by default) and wait longer between tries if you're unreachable. If their workspace is offline when you add records, they arrive the next time it fetches. Fetching twice does no harm.
