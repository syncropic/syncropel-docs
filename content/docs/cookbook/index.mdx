## What this section is

A recipe is not a tutorial or a guide. A tutorial teaches an idea; a guide explains one part of the product. A recipe solves one problem in one way. You arrive, copy the commands, and leave.

Every recipe:

1. **Names a problem** in one sentence at the top.
2. **Gives the commands** you can paste at a terminal after changing the names.
3. **Explains the one trade-off** to know before you rely on it.
4. **Links to the background** if you want to know why it works.

Recipes stay short on purpose. A longer topic belongs in a guide.

## How these relate to guides

Guides explain one part of the product from start to finish ([CEL expressions](/docs/guides/cel-expressions), [Federation pair](/docs/guides/federation-pair), [Task management](/docs/guides/task-management)). Recipes combine pieces from several parts to solve one practical problem.

## If a recipe doesn't work

If a recipe's commands don't work when you follow them, tell us: a broken recipe is worse than none.
