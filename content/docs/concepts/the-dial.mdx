The Dial is Syncropel's most distinctive innovation — a single continuous parameter on the interval \[0, 1\] that controls how the system balances between **exact replay** of known patterns and **novel generation** of new solutions.

## Why One Parameter?

Most systems force a binary choice: either run a fixed script or let an AI improvise. The Dial replaces this with a continuous spectrum. You express *how much autonomy* you're willing to grant, and the system responds accordingly.

This is **Axiom A8**: a single scalar that interpolates between deterministic and generative behavior.

## The Spectrum

| Range | Mode | Behavior | Trust | Cost |
|-------|------|----------|-------|------|
| **0.0 – 0.1** | REPLAY | Exact pattern match — fastest, most predictable | Highest | Lowest |
| **0.1 – 0.4** | ADAPT | Adapt existing patterns to your goal | High | Low |
| **0.4 – 0.7** | EXPLORE | Explore variations and alternatives | Medium | Medium |
| **0.7 – 1.0** | CREATE | Generate novel traces — most creative | Lower | Higher |

## How It Works

Under the hood, the Dial controls **diffusion depth** in the Generative Trace Model (GTM):

- **Dial = 0**: Pure replay. The system finds an exact match in the pattern registry and replays it. Zero generation, zero risk, zero cost.
- **Dial < 1/3**: Parameter exploration. The system matches a known pattern but explores different parameter values (one diffusion step).
- **Dial < 2/3**: Operation exploration. The system matches the shape of a pattern but may substitute operations (two diffusion steps).
- **Dial ≥ 2/3**: Full generation. The system generates novel traces from intent alone (three diffusion steps).

## The Trust-Dial Feedback Loop

The Dial doesn't exist in isolation — it interacts with the **trust score** of patterns:

- **High trust (> 0.9)** → System recommends low Dial (< 1/3) → Deterministic, cheap, fast
- **Low trust (< 0.3)** → System recommends high Dial (> 2/3) → More careful, more creative, more expensive

As patterns prove themselves through successful execution, trust increases and the Dial naturally drifts toward zero — the system gets cheaper and more reliable over time. This is **Axiom A2** (Convergence) in action.

## Example

```
Goal: "Find customers at risk of churn based on purchase frequency"

Dial = 0.0  →  Exact replay of a crystallized churn-analysis pattern
                Cost: $0.00 | Latency: 200ms | Auditability: 100%

Dial = 0.5  →  Adapt the churn pattern with different feature weights
                Cost: $0.001 | Latency: 2s | Auditability: 70%

Dial = 1.0  →  Generate a novel approach from scratch using available data
                Cost: $0.01 | Latency: 10s | Auditability: 30%
```

## Design Philosophy

The Dial embodies a core Syncropel principle: **bounded autonomy**. Rather than choosing between "human does everything" and "AI does everything," the Dial gives you a knob. You decide the tradeoff. The system respects your decision with mathematical precision.

> "Like gravity cannot be bypassed, bribed, or forgotten, neither can the constraints encoded in the Dial."
