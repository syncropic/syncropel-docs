The Dial is a single continuous parameter on the interval \[0, 1\] that controls how much autonomy a session has. Low values replay known patterns exactly. High values generate novel solutions. The system enforces this with mathematical precision — not guidelines, not guardrails, but structural bounds that cannot be bypassed.

## Zones

The Dial divides into zones at exact fractional boundaries. These thresholds are permanent and will never change.

| Zone | Range | What Happens |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **REPLAY** | \[0, 1/3) | Exact pattern match from the registry. Zero generation, zero risk, lowest cost. |
| **ADAPT** | \[1/3, 1/2) | Adapt a known pattern — modify parameters, keep structure. |
| **EXPLORE** | \[1/2, 2/3) | Vary operations and discover alternatives. Hybrid approach. |
| **CREATE** | \[2/3, 1\] | Full generation from intent. Most creative, highest cost. |

The thresholds are **exact fractions**: T1 = 1/3, T2 = 1/2, T3 = 2/3. Not 0.25, not 0.33, not 0.75. The fractions are the spec.

```
0         1/3        1/2        2/3         1
|─────────|──────────|──────────|───────────|
  REPLAY     ADAPT     EXPLORE     CREATE
```

## How Trust Controls the Dial

You don't pick any dial position you want. Your **trust score** determines a **dial ceiling** — the maximum position you're allowed to request.

| Trust Score | Dial Ceiling | Zones Available |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| \< 0.30 | 1/3 | REPLAY only |
| \< 0.50 | 1/2 | REPLAY + ADAPT |
| \< 0.70 | 2/3 | REPLAY + ADAPT + EXPLORE |
| &ge; 0.70 | 1.0 | All zones |

**New principals start with a default starting trust**, giving a trust score of approximately 0.47. This places them in the second tier: REPLAY + ADAPT. They can use known patterns and adapt them, but must earn trust before exploring or creating.

Trust grows through successful observations and decays over time. Use a pattern successfully, your trust increases. Stop using it, your trust decays back toward the default baseline. Reliable behavior is rewarded with more autonomy.

## Hash Access by Zone

The dial zone also determines which hash levels the session can access:

| Zone | Hash Access | What This Means |
|------|------------|-----------------|
| REPLAY | L0 | Exact replay only — you see full detail |
| ADAPT | L0, L1 | Can match structural patterns (strip parameters) |
| EXPLORE | L0, L1, L2 | Can match flow patterns (strip operations) |
| CREATE | L0, L1, L2, L3 | Can match intent patterns (shapes only) |

Higher dial positions unlock coarser hash levels, enabling broader pattern discovery. But L0 hashes **never leave your namespace** regardless of zone.

## Governance Tiers

The dial determines the governance tier for the session:

| Tier | Dial Range | Additional Enforcement |
|------|-----------|----------------------|
| **STANDARD** | d \< 2/3 | Normal capability + budget + structural checks |
| **ELEVATED** | d &ge; 2/3 | All standard checks **plus** HITL (human-in-the-loop) approval required for PUT and CALL effects |

In the CREATE zone, writing data or invoking external services requires explicit human approval. The system doesn't just warn — it blocks execution until approval is granted.

## The Feedback Loop

The dial creates a virtuous cycle:

```
Execute effect → Record observation → Update trust score
                                           ↓
                    Trust grows → Dial ceiling rises
                                           ↓
                    More autonomy → Better patterns discovered
                                           ↓
                    Better patterns → Higher success rate → back to trust grows
```

Over time, reliable workflows naturally drift toward lower dial positions — cheaper, faster, more deterministic. Novel workflows start in CREATE and crystallize into REPLAY as they prove themselves. This is the convergence guarantee.

## Design Philosophy

The Dial embodies **bounded autonomy**. Rather than choosing between "human does everything" and "AI does everything," you express how much freedom you're willing to grant. The system enforces your choice structurally — not through a safety filter that can be bypassed, but through mathematical constraints baked into the governance token.

> Like gravity, the Dial cannot be bypassed, bribed, or forgotten.
