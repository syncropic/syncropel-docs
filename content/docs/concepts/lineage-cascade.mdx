The Lineage Cascade is Syncropel's mechanism for **automatic, physics-based value attribution**. When work is derived from other work, the system traces the chain of creation and distributes value to every contributor — automatically, atomically, and without intermediaries.

## The Problem

In today's digital world, creation is rarely isolated. A DJ remixes a track. A data scientist builds on a colleague's feature engineering. A developer forks and extends a library. But the original creators rarely see value from downstream work.

Why? Because attribution is manual, unenforceable, and easily lost.

## The Solution: Physics, Not Promises

Syncropel makes attribution a **physical law** of the system, not a policy that can be ignored:

1. **Every output references its inputs** — cryptographic lineage hashes link derived work to source material
2. **Value flows are atomic** — when a derived trace settles, all upstream creators are paid in the same transaction
3. **Conservation applies** — total value in = total value out + costs (Axiom A4). Value cannot appear or vanish.

## How It Works

```
Creator A produces Trace₁ (original work)
    │
    ▼
Creator B produces Trace₂ (derived from Trace₁)
    │
    ▼
Creator C produces Trace₃ (derived from Trace₂)
    │
    ▼
End user pays $1.00 for Trace₃
    │
    ├── Creator C receives $0.60 (direct value)
    ├── Creator B receives $0.25 (lineage: Trace₂ → Trace₃)
    ├── Creator A receives $0.10 (lineage: Trace₁ → Trace₂ → Trace₃)
    └── Protocol fee:       $0.05
```

The split ratios are determined by **Covenants** — rules bound to assets that enforce themselves. When Creator A publishes Trace₁, they attach a Covenant specifying their terms. These terms cascade through all derivative work.

## Key Properties

- **Automatic**: No manual tracking, no invoices, no reconciliation
- **Atomic**: Settlement happens in one transaction — work and payment are one
- **Cryptographic**: Lineage hashes are tamper-proof; attribution cannot be forged
- **Composable**: Multiple lineage chains can merge and split
- **Auditable**: Every payment can be traced to its source through the lineage graph

## The DJ Analogy

Imagine a DJ who creates a remix:

1. They sample a vocal from Track A (Covenant: 15% of downstream revenue)
2. They use a beat from Track B (Covenant: 10% of downstream revenue)
3. They publish the remix as Track C with their own Covenant

When Track C earns revenue:
- The DJ gets their share
- The vocalist from Track A gets 15% automatically
- The beat producer from Track B gets 10% automatically
- No lawyers. No disputes. No delays. Physics.
