## How Trust Works

Trust in Syncropel is computed automatically from your track record — not assigned by an administrator.

### Trust grows with success

Every successful action adds evidence to your trust score. The more reliable you are in a domain, the higher your trust.

### Trust decays over time

Trust is time-sensitive. Recent evidence matters more than old evidence. If you haven't used a pattern in a while, your trust in that area decreases.

### Trust controls autonomy

Your trust score directly affects what you can do:

| Trust Level | What You Can Do |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Low (new user) | Basic operations, limited autonomy |
| Medium | Standard operations, moderate autonomy |
| High | Advanced operations, high autonomy |
| Very high | Full autonomy, creative exploration |

### New users start with moderate trust

You don't start at zero — the system gives new users enough trust to be productive immediately, then adjusts based on evidence.

### Trust is per-domain

Trust is computed separately for different types of work. You might have high trust in "code editing" but low trust in "deployment." Each domain tracks independently.
