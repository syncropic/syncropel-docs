## Overview

The local workspace at [syncropel.com/local](https://syncropel.com/local) gives you a full dashboard connected to your `spl serve` daemon on localhost:9100. No account required — if your daemon is running, the workspace connects automatically.

**Open it:**

```bash
spl serve --open
```

Or navigate directly to [syncropel.com/local](https://syncropel.com/local).

## What you see

The workspace shows everything flowing through your proxy in real-time:

- **Threads** — groups of related LLM calls (sidebar, left panel)
- **Records** — every API call with model, tokens, cost, latency (detail panel)
- **Actors** — who made the call (your app, an agent, a script)
- **Trust scores** — reliability metrics computed from your usage history
- **Governance** — policies, audit trail, signing keys

## How it works

The workspace at syncropel.com is a hosted web app (Cloudflare Pages). When you visit `/local`, it:

1. Probes `localhost:9100/health` to detect your daemon
2. If the daemon is running — connects and shows the full workspace
3. If the daemon is not running — shows a "Start your daemon" page with instructions
4. Auto-retries every 3 seconds until the daemon is detected

All data stays on your machine. The web app makes API calls directly from your browser to `localhost:9100`. Nothing is sent to Syncropel's servers.

## Navigation

| Page | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| `/local/dashboard` | Overview with active threads |
| `/local/threads` | Thread browser with search |
| `/local/actors` | Actor directory with trust scores |
| `/local/hub` | Record browser with filters |
| `/local/governance` | Trust scores, audit trail, policies |
| `/local/governance/trust` | Trust scores per actor per domain |
| `/local/models` | Model usage statistics |

## Status bar

The bottom bar shows:
- **Connection status** — green dot when connected to localhost:9100
- **Record count** — total records in your database
- **Pattern count** — extracted patterns
- **Namespace** — current namespace (default: "all namespaces")

## "Running locally" banner

A banner at the top shows your connection status and a link to sign in. Signing in is optional — it enables syncing your records across devices via the hub.

## Offline support

The workspace is a Progressive Web App (PWA). After your first visit, it caches in your browser and works even without internet — as long as your daemon is running on localhost:9100.

## Legacy console

The old console at `localhost:9100/console` now redirects to `syncropel.com/local`. If you have no internet, the redirect page shows a link to check daemon health at `/health`.
