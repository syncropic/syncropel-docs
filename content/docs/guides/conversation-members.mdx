## Overview

Every conversation has a members page. It answers one question first, **Who can
read this**, and then everything around it: who's invited, the other assistants
in the workspace, guest links, when what's here was opened, and whose access has
ended.

Open it from the people shown at the top of a conversation.

## Who can read this

This section is always first, and it can't be hidden. It lists:

- **you**, first;
- the people who can read the conversation;
- the assistants that are in it, each with a line saying what it does here, such
  as **answers messages here** or **answers when named here**;
- guests, because guests can read it too.

A row doesn't say whether someone is online or when they were last here.

Press a person's or an assistant's row to open its details. On a phone the details
open from the bottom of the screen; on a wider screen they open beside the list.

An assistant that answers here has an **on** / **off** switch on its row. It
applies to this conversation only: switching an assistant off here doesn't change
it anywhere else.

## Invited

People and offices that have been invited but haven't joined yet. They can't
read the conversation until they join, so they're listed here and not under Who
can read this. An office or assistant invited by name reads **invited, can read
once they join**.

## Other assistants in this workspace

The workspace's other assistants, folded to one line until you open it. They don't
answer here unless you name one: "Name one in a message here to ask it."

When the workspace doesn't say which assistants answer in this conversation, the
section says so: "This workspace doesn't say which of these answer here."

## Guests and links

The links that let guests in, including a public link. Each link's **⋯** offers
**copy the link**, **show the QR code**, and, if you may, **change access** and
**turn the link off**.

## Opened here

When what's in this conversation was opened, over the last 30 days. The newest
days come first, and **Show all** with the number of days lists every one.

What you see depends on who you are:

- **The owner** sees the conversation as a whole, headed **Opened here in the
  last 30 days**, with exact numbers: a day reads like "Oct 12 · read by 3
  people", and the total like "14 reads in 30 days".
- **Anyone else** sees only when what they wrote or added was opened, headed
  "What you wrote or added here was opened…". To protect the people reading, a
  small number isn't given exactly: a day can read "read by fewer than 3 people",
  or "read by at least" a number when the count is a lower bound.

Days are named by their date, not "today".

## Access ended

Only someone who can end other people's access sees this section, folded to one
line. A row reads like "access ended Oct 6". It covers someone removed, a guest
whose access ran out and a link that expired alike, so it doesn't say which.

Someone who was erased is never listed here, and isn't counted.

## Arrange the page

The members page is arranged the same way for every conversation, and the
arrangement is yours alone: it's kept on your own devices, and nobody else sees
it. Press **Edit page** to move a section with **Move up** and **Move down**,
**Remove** one, or put one back with **Add a section**. **Who can read this** is
"Always here. You can make it smaller." Press **Done** when you're finished.

Hiding a section only changes your view. Hiding **Opened here** doesn't stop
reads being counted.

## Roles

A role gives everyone who holds it the same conversations. The owner manages
roles on the workspace's members page, under **Roles**.

### Make a role

1. Under **Roles**, start a **New role**.
2. Give it a **Name**: "Lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores."
3. Under **Conversations**, choose each conversation and whether holders **can
   read it** or **can read and write**.
4. Press **Create role**.

Each role's row says how many conversations it has and how many people hold it.
After you save, the Studio may list what else your change affects, under **This
also changes:**.

To delete a role, the Studio asks "Delete the role" and the role's name, then
**Delete**.

### Change someone's role

On a person's row, press **Change role…**, choose the role and press **Change
role**. Before you do, the Studio says what changes:

- "A role sets which conversations Maya can see. Conversations they were added
  to directly stay."
- If they can see everything in the workspace today, it warns that the role will
  narrow that, and that the only way to give them everything again is to remove
  them and invite them again.

A person's role and their offices show under their name.

### When a role is in the way

If someone is in a conversation through their role, you can't take them out of
that one conversation. The Studio says so, for example: "Maya is in this
conversation through the role “Finance”. Change their role or the role's
conversations instead. Nothing was changed."

## Leave a workspace

On the workspace's members page, your own row has **leave**. The Studio asks
"Leave this workspace? This browser's key stops working on its next request; what
you wrote stays." Press **yes, leave**, or **stay**.

## After someone is erased

When a person is erased from the workspace, each of their messages reads "This
message was erased." wherever it appears.

## Common gotchas

- **A number that says "at least".** That's the privacy floor at work, not a
  missing read. Only the owner sees exact counts.
- **An assistant that moved sections.** The assistants in the conversation are
  the ones that answer here, were named here or have worked here. The rest wait
  under Other assistants until you name one.
