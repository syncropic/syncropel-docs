## Overview

When an app asks to connect to your workspace, the Studio shows you what it's
asking for before anything is given. Nothing is allowed until you press
**Allow**, and an app can't use your connections, such as your GitHub or your
email, unless you check them.

## Before you allow

The page is headed with the app and your workspace, for example "Notes app wants
to connect to Acme". It says:

- where the app comes from: "From notes.example.com";
- where you'll be sent afterwards, and to continue only if you started this from
  there;
- **It will be able to:** what the app asks to do, such as reading everything you
  can see in the workspace, or adding messages and notes as you;
- what it can never do: "It can't change who has access, delete your workspace,
  or see your passkeys and keys.";
- how long it stays connected, and that "You can end it any time from Where
  you're signed in."

### Your connections

If you have connections the app could use, they're listed under "It can't use
any of your connections unless you check them here:". Each one is unchecked.
Check only the ones you want this app to use.

If the app could use some before, those are checked again, and the page says so:
"Uncheck any you don't want it to use."

Press **Allow**, or **Don't allow**. Allowing may ask for your passkey.

## Which app can use a connection

Each connection lists the apps and keys that may use it, and how:

- "Notes app can read with it", or "can read and make changes with it";
- "until" a date, where the access ends;
- "Notes app isn't signed in anymore, so it can't use this." or "Notes app was
  disconnected, so it can't use this." when it can't use it any more.

### After the update

Apps and keys you'd already allowed before this update can still use all your
connections. Until you choose, they're marked **Can use all your connections**,
and a notice says: "Apps and agents you'd already allowed can still use all your
connections. You can now choose which ones each may use."

- Press **Choose which** on an app to pick its connections.
- Press **Keep all** to leave every one as it is.

### Keys you keep in the workspace

A key you gave the workspace for a connection says who can read it:

- "Kept by this workspace, on a computer Syncropic manages. Syncropic can read
  it."
- "Kept by this workspace, on its own computer."

## See what an app did

In [Where you're signed in](/docs/guides/where-youre-signed-in), each app's row
counts what it did with your connections and your workspace:

- "Used your connections" and how many times;
- "Wasn't allowed" and how many times, only when something was refused;
- "Read" a number of "records in the last 30 days";
- "Nothing in the last 30 days" when it did nothing in that time.

These are counts only. They never name what was read.

## Common gotchas

- **An app that worked before and now can't use a connection.** Check that its
  connection is checked, and that the app is still signed in.
- **"Where this app comes from wasn't checked."** The page couldn't confirm where
  the app comes from. Only allow it if you know you started this yourself.
