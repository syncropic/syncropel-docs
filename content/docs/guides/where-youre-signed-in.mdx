---
title: Where you're signed in
description: See every device and browser signed in to a workspace as you, and sign out one you don't use or don't recognize.
---

Every browser and app you use with a workspace signs in on its own. That means
you can see each one, and end one without touching the others. If you lose a
phone, you can sign it out from any other device.

All of this is in the Studio under **You**, in the **Devices and keys** section
for the workspace you're in. Only you see your own devices: the workspace owner
can't see a member's devices here.

## Your devices

**Your devices** lists each device signed in to this workspace as you, with when
it was first seen and when it was last used. The device you're on reads **This
device** and comes first.

If no other device is signed in, the list says **Only this device is signed in.**

The button to add another device sits under this list. See
[Add a device](/docs/guides/add-a-device).

### Sign a device out

1. On the device's row, press **Sign out**.
2. The Studio asks "Sign out" and the device's name, and reminds you it'll need
   to sign in again with a code from a device that's signed in. Press **Sign
   out**, or **Cancel**.
3. Confirm with your passkey when asked.

The device is signed out of every browser tab and app on it, at once. Its row
moves to the end of the list and reads **Ended** with the date, **from this
browser**.

If you have no passkey and signed in more than 5 minutes ago, the Studio says
**To sign out another device without a passkey, confirm it's you first.** Press
**Confirm it's you** and follow the steps in
[Keeping your access](/docs/guides/keeping-your-access#if-youre-asked-to-confirm-its-you-first).
This shows straight away on the row, because a lost or stolen phone is exactly
when you need it.

## Where you're signed in

Below your devices, **Where you're signed in** lists each sign-in on its own: for
example, the Studio on Chrome and an app on the same laptop are two rows. Each
row shows what signed in, on which device, when it signed in and when it was
last used. The one you're using reads **This browser**.

A row can also say how much that sign-in did in the last 30 days, such as how
many rooms it read and items it wrote. It counts them and never names them, so
the list is safe to show on a shared screen.

- Press **End** on a row to end that one sign-in.
- Press **End every other session** to end all of them except this browser.

Ending one may ask for your passkey. If this browser has no passkey, the sheet
offers **Use a recovery code** instead. Your recovery key then works once, so
you'll make a new one afterward in Devices and keys.

Ended rows stay on the list for a while and say how they ended, for example
**Ended** on a date **from this browser**, **from another of your devices**, or
**you signed out**.

Anything a sign-in writes is written as you.

## When you've been signed out

If a sign-in ends while you're using it, the Studio tells you why, for example:

- you signed out;
- it was ended from another of your devices;
- it wasn't used for 7 days, or reached its 30-day limit.

Press **Sign in again** to carry on.

If someone got back into the workspace as you using a recovery key or a new
passkey, the Studio says **You were signed out here**, with when it happened and
a deadline. If that wasn't you, sign in with a passkey you already had before the
deadline, and remove the one they used in Devices and keys.

## On a shared computer

When you join a workspace by pasting an invite into **+ add a connection** on a
browser that isn't yours, tick **This is a shared computer** before you press
**accept invite**. Then reloading or closing the
tab signs you out, and so do 30 minutes without use or 8 hours in all. Nothing
about you is kept on that browser.

## Common questions

**Does signing out a device remove my passkey?**
No. Your passkeys and recovery key are listed separately, under
[Ways back in](/docs/guides/keeping-your-access#ways-back-in).

**Can I sign out the device I'm on from here?**
No. The **Sign out** button on this list is for your other devices, so the row
for **This device** doesn't have one.

**I don't recognize a device.**
Sign it out, then check **Ways back in** for a passkey you didn't add, and remove
it.
