## Overview

A passkey or a recovery code gets you back in when you lose one device. Guardians
are for the worst case: you've lost every device. Each guardian keeps one part of
a way back into your account. It takes several of them, and then a wait you can
stop, so no single guardian, and nobody who tricks one, can get in on their own.

Guardians don't see anything in your workspace.

## Add a guardian

Guardians are set up on your **You** page, under **Guardians**: "People you trust
to help you back in if you lose every device. Each keeps one part; it takes
several of them, then a wait you can stop."

1. Press **Add a guardian**.
2. Enter **Their email** and **What to call them** ("Only you see this.").
3. Press **Send the invite**, or **Make their invite link** to send the link
   yourself. A link works once, and you only see it now: "Send this link to
   them yourself, somewhere only they'll see it."

Each guardian's row says where they are: **Invited, not accepted yet**,
**Accepted**, or **Holding their part**. The section also says how many are needed,
for example "2 of your 3 guardians are needed."

### Remove a guardian

Press **Remove** on their row. Removing someone doesn't take their part back on
its own: it keeps working with the others' until you give your remaining
guardians new parts. That's done from the command line today, and the Studio
shows the commands to run.

## Being someone's guardian

When someone asks you to be their guardian, you get a link. It opens a page that
says **Be a guardian for** and the place.

- Press **Accept and keep my part here**. Your part is kept in that browser, so
  use a browser you'll keep: "if its data is cleared, your part is gone and
  they'll need to ask you again."
- Come back to this page if they ask you to help them get back in.

## Get back in with your guardians

If you've lost every device, open **Get back into your account** in a browser
you'll keep using, not a private window.

1. Enter **Your workspace's address**, like jane.syncropel.app, and press **Get
   back in**. Nothing is sent until you press it.
2. The page gives you a link to send to each guardian, or a square they can scan,
   and a **Check**: 13 letters and numbers.
3. Call each guardian, or meet them, and read them the check. They type it before
   they approve. A guardian should never approve a check that only arrived by
   message or email.
4. Press **See how many have approved** to follow along. Once enough guardians
   have approved, there's a wait, in case it wasn't you.
5. When the wait is over, choose a signing key to bring onto this browser and
   press **Bring back**.
6. The page asks **Keep this browser as one of your devices?** Press **Keep it**,
   or **Just this time** if you'll sign out when you're done.
7. Press **Go to** and your workspace's name.

Some keys can't come back in a browser. The page says so and tells you to use
`spl` on a computer instead.

### What your guardian sees

The link you send opens the guardian's page. They type **The check they read to
you**. If it doesn't match, the page says "That isn't the check for this request.
Don't approve." and nothing is sent. If it matches, they press **Approve this
device** and confirm.

## When someone else tries

Whenever a recovery starts, your **You** page and an email both say **Someone is
trying to get into your account**, with how many guardians have approved and
when it completes. If it isn't you, press **This isn't me. Stop it.** The Studio
says "Stopped. No one got in."

## Common gotchas

- **A clock that's off.** If a device's clock is more than five minutes off, the
  page asks you to set it to the right time and try again.
- **A cleared browser part way through.** Start again, and ask your guardians
  again.
