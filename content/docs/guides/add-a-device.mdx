title: Add a device
description: Sign in on a new phone or laptop by scanning a code from a device that's already signed in, or join a workspace from an invite link in your browser.

You can sign in to a workspace on a new device without a terminal and without
asking anyone for a new invite. A device that's already signed in shows a code,
the new device scans it, and you confirm with two words.

If you run the workspace yourself and prefer the command line, see
[Pair, share, invite](/docs/guides/pair-share-invite).

## Add a phone or another device

You need a device that's already signed in to the workspace, and the new device
with a camera.

1. On the signed-in device, open **You**, go to **Devices and keys**, and press
   **Add a phone or another device** under **Your devices**.
2. Confirm with your passkey when asked.
3. The Studio shows **Scan this with the device you're adding** and a code. It
   works once, for 2 minutes. Only scan it on a device you own.
4. Scan the code with the new device. It opens the Studio and shows **Your two
   words**. Don't read them out to anyone.
5. Back on the first device, the Studio asks **Which two words does your other
   device show?** and offers four pairs. Choose the pair the new device shows.

The first device says your other device is signed in, and the new device says
**You're signed in.** and opens the workspace. It now appears in
[Your devices](/docs/guides/where-youre-signed-in#your-devices).

Right after this, the new device offers to add a passkey without asking you to
confirm it's you again. See
[Add a passkey](/docs/guides/keeping-your-access#add-a-passkey).

### If the words don't match

If none of the four pairs is the one on the new device, press **None of these**.
Choosing a wrong pair or **None of these** cancels the sign-in: nothing is
signed in and the code stops working. The new device says the sign-in was
cancelled. Press **Make a new code** to start again.

The two words are what make this safe. Someone who photographs your code from
across the room can scan it, but they'd see different words from the ones you'd
pick.

### If a second device scans the code

A code works for one device only. If a second device scans it, the Studio says a
device you may not own tried to use it, so it no longer works and nothing was
signed in. Make a new code, and keep it where only you can see it.

### If the code expires

After 2 minutes the Studio says **This code expired. Make a new one to try
again.** Press **Make a new code**.

## Join from an invite link

When someone sends you an invite link to a workspace, open it in your browser.

1. The workspace's own invite page tells you what the invite is for. Press
   **Accept** {/* WORDS? story to confirm: this button is on the workspace's own invite page, not the Studio; check its live label */}.
2. The Studio opens and accepts the invite for this browser. If it's a member
   invite that doesn't name you yet, pick your member name and press **accept
   invite**.
3. You land signed in on what you were invited to: the conversation the invite
   is for, or everything shared with you.

If the workspace records who reads what, the Studio tells you once, on a **you're
in** screen, before you go in. Press **go in** to carry on.

This browser now has its own sign-in to the workspace, listed under
[Where you're signed in](/docs/guides/where-youre-signed-in). Because you just
joined, you can add a passkey straight away.

### Paste an invite instead

You can also paste an invite link into **+ add a connection**. The Studio shows
what the invite gives you. Press **accept invite** to join. On a computer that
isn't yours, tick **This is a shared computer** first, so closing the tab signs
you out.

### If you joined from another browser before

If you're a guest and you open the same invite in a different browser, the
Studio says **You joined from another browser**. To carry on as before, open the
invite in the browser you used last time.
